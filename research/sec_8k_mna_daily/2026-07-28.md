### INTC - 企业范围运营重组、非核心业务退出、房地产整合及Mobileye股份出售

- 状态: 重组已启动并正在实施；相关行动预计于2025年第四季度基本完成；Mobileye股份出售已于2025年7月完成
- 分类: 业务重组, 资产剥离
- 交易对手: Mobileye
- 标的/资产: Intel核心员工队伍、低优先级业务和项目、部分房地产物业、德国和波兰计划项目、哥斯达黎加装配与测试业务，以及Mobileye 57.5百万股Class A股份
- 置信度: 0.99
- SEC URL: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/50863/000005086325000107/intc-20250724.htm
- 对价/费用: 重组预计确认19亿美元重组费用，其中2025年第二季度确认18亿美元；出售Mobileye股份为Intel资产变现，增加资产负债表约9.22亿美元
> As part of this transformation, we implemented the 2025 Restructuring Plan to lower expenses, streamline our organizational structure and reduce management layers across functions while reallocating resources toward our core client and server businesses by reducing investment in lower-priority programs and initiatives.
> The Company expects these headcount reduction initiatives will reduce our core Intel workforce by 15% by the end of fiscal 2025.
> As a result of initiating and deploying the 2025 Restructuring Plan, the Company expects to recognize $1.9 billion of restructuring charges related to these actions, of which the Company is recognizing $1.8 billion in the second quarter of 2025.
> In line with Intel’s commitment to strengthen its balance sheet and monetize non-core assets, the company sold, via a secondary offering, 57.5 million of net Class A shares of Mobileye in July 2025, adding approximately $922 million to the balance sheet.
- 待核实: 文件未披露德国和波兰项目、哥斯达黎加业务整合及部分房地产退出的单独交易对手方或单独对价。; 文件将Mobileye股份出售描述为二次发行，但未披露具体承销商或购买方。
