# Oatly historical research workbook

As of: 2026-10-20. File: `OTLY_model.xlsx`.

Coverage: FY2021–FY2025 and 2024Q3–2026Q2; 12 English-language sheets covering statements, segments, operating disclosures, historical derived metrics and material notes. The source set comprises five original annual 20-F reports and eight selected interim financial-report / earnings-exhibit views filed under 6-K. USD thousands unless a section specifies another unit.

Reported values use original HTML cells or exact original prose. Comparable panels prefer the latest filed whole-period classification. Native schedules retain the source-year version and their own date/category axes; later comparatives take precedence when a prior year was revised. Legacy and recast segment axes are separate. Filed cash flows remain YTD; discrete-quarter cash flows and TTM measures use formulas. Ordinary-share, historical ADS and restated ADS quantities retain their stated units.

Operating layout updated 2026-10-21: Segments uses separate regional headings with revenue and EBITDA/account subgroups, including SBC for every reporting scope. Revenue and Operations separates consolidated/regional sales channels, customer-country revenue, the EBITDA reconciliation and operating volumes. Revenue Bridges uses comparable annual/discrete-quarter metric matrices, with cumulative disclosures in a separately labelled YTD panel. Identical revenue columns and repeated filing panels share a single displayed owner while retaining every original source locator. FY2022 filed FX impact is an add-back, whereas subsequent filings generally deduct the filed FX impact; source signs are retained.

Operating IC deducts identified cash/investment/financing assets and the disclosed FY2023 equipment-return refund receivable. Operating NWC excludes tax, identified financing prepayments, that refund receivable and the known FY2022 disposal group. Current FX hedges remain where the issuer does not split operating/financing purpose. Other receivables are not represented as fully purified. NOPAT uses signed actual group tax. Annual/TTM turnover days use actual calendar days. Returns requiring an unavailable one-year-ago capital balance remain blank; reported dashes in financial amount columns represent nil.

The FY2023 equipment-return receivable is 12,200 in USD thousands, converted from the issuer's rounded USD12.2 million disclosure; it is not a more precise reported amount. Co-packer volume-shortfall expense is shown as annual / filed YTD expense, not a forecast of commitments.
