# Guidewire Software -- Business and Offering Handbook

## 1. Company Overview and Mission

Guidewire Software (NYSE: GWRE) is the platform that property and casualty (P&C) insurers trust to engage, innovate, and grow efficiently. Founded in 2001 by six entrepreneurs with a vision to transform P&C insurance, the company is headquartered in San Mateo, California, and serves more than 570 insurers in 43 countries worldwide [O0001] [O0012].

The company is 100% focused on the P&C insurance vertical, with approximately 2,500 employees committed to customer success [O0001]. Guidewire has completed over 1,700 successful implementations ranging from single-system deployments to greenfield builds, core replacements, and rapid product launches [O0001]. The company boasts the largest partner ecosystem in P&C insurance, with 235 Marketplace partners offering over 385 extensions, and more than 31,000 practice consultants trained or experienced in Guidewire products globally [O0001].

Guidewire went public on the New York Stock Exchange in 2012 under the ticker GWRE [O0001]. The company operates under three core values: Integrity (truthful relationships), Rationality (clear arguments and evidence-based decisions), and Collegiality (working together as professional equals) [O0001].

## 2. Core Products

Guidewire's core product portfolio comprises two main platforms -- InsuranceSuite (for mid-size and large insurers) and InsuranceNow (for regional and super-regional insurers and MGAs) -- along with a set of purpose-built applications called Guidewire Centers [O0003].

### 2.1 InsuranceSuite

InsuranceSuite is the company's flagship core platform for P&C insurers, bringing together PolicyCenter, ClaimCenter, and BillingCenter through Guidewire Cloud to power the full insurance lifecycle. It is built to give mid-sized and large insurers the flexibility and scale they need for connected operations. More than 300 P&C customers worldwide run on InsuranceSuite [O0004] [O0003].

InsuranceSuite is available globally, supports multi-currency and multi-language operations, and is typically adopted by insurers with direct written premiums (DWP) of $300M and above, though customers below $100M also use it [O0003].

### 2.2 PolicyCenter

PolicyCenter manages the full policy lifecycle -- from quote to renewal -- in one connected system. It provides flexible configuration tools that let insurers model and deploy new products quickly, and integrates with billing and claims to keep data consistent across the business [O0003].

### 2.3 ClaimCenter

ClaimCenter handles the entire claims process from first notice of loss to settlement. It features built-in automation and intelligence to streamline assignment, fraud detection, and adjudication, giving adjusters a complete view of every claim for faster resolution and better claimant experiences [O0003].

### 2.4 BillingCenter

BillingCenter supports every type of billing process, including direct, agency, installment, and usage-based payments. Built-in automation and digital invoicing help insurers reduce errors, accelerate receivables, and expand payment options for policyholders [O0003].

### 2.5 PricingCenter

PricingCenter provides a unified pricing and rating environment that leverages explainable AI and advanced analytics. It enables actuaries and IT to instantly model new risks, validate them against portfolios, and deploy precise, profitable rates to stay ahead of competition [O0003].

### 2.6 UnderwritingCenter

UnderwritingCenter shifts underwriting from a static, wait-and-process approach to an intelligent, dynamic, event-driven workflow. It automatically guides submissions through the underwriting lifecycle, minimizing manual processes and freeing underwriters to focus on judgment-driven decisions and profitable deal structuring [O0003].

### 2.7 InsuranceNow

InsuranceNow is an end-to-end platform built for regional and super-regional insurers and MGAs. It comes pre-configured with lines of business so teams can launch quickly and grow with confidence. InsuranceNow is US-only, single-currency, and English-only, typically serving insurers with DWP of $300M and below, though some customers exceed $1B [O0003] [O0003].

## 3. Analytics Products

Guidewire Analytics is a portfolio of insurance analytics products that embed insights directly into policy and claims workflows. As of the official site data, 328 customers (insurers, reinsurers, and brokers) worldwide use Guidewire Analytics products [O0005] [O0005].

### 3.1 HazardHub

HazardHub is a property risk data platform that provides risk scores and grades for any U.S. address, covering more than 1,400 risk factors including hurricanes, wildfires, superfund contamination, and crime. It delivers property risk insights to core systems in seconds. HazardHub data is available for purchase worldwide but currently returns data for U.S. and Canada addresses only [O0005]. HazardHub was acquired in 2021 [O0001].

### 3.2 Predict

Predict is Guidewire's predictive analytics product, voted Predictive Analytics Solution of the Year by the 2023 Data Breakthrough Awards. It enables insurers to build and import any model and deploy it into core systems in days instead of months. It monitors and measures model impact on risk selection, pricing, claims triage, settlement, and more [O0005]. Users of Predict have seen up to 20% growth in written premium for underwriters [O0008].

### 3.3 Data Studio

Data Studio transforms raw insurance data into curated, analysis-ready datasets with P&C insurance data models for policy, claims, and billing. It connects seamlessly with Explore, Predict, or third-party BI tools and delivers actionable insights across the enterprise. Data Studio is available to all Guidewire Cloud customers and supports global insurers [O0005].

### 3.4 Cyence

Cyence provides sophisticated cyber risk modeling for insurers, reinsurers, and ratings agencies. It scans more than 1,000 data points from 400 sources to assess and quantify cybersecurity risk for individual companies and large portfolios, enabling confident underwriting, profitable pricing, effective risk transfer, and customer retention [O0005] [O0005].

### 3.5 Canvas

Canvas empowers claims management and catastrophe response teams by geo-visualizing policyholder locations and related claims. It enhances data with third-party data to optimize CAT response and management, and is available for rapid deployment to ClaimCenter customers [O0005].

### 3.6 Compare

Compare enables insurers to monitor claims performance continuously and benchmark against industry peers using aggregated, anonymized, and secure claims data. It analyzes ClaimCenter data on a real-time dashboard to improve indemnity, cycle times, reserves, salvage, subrogation, and more [O0005].

### 3.7 Explore

Explore is an AI-powered business intelligence tool that improves operational efficiency across the organization. Policy teams use Explore dashboards to review sales, service, and submission progress, while claims managers monitor cycle times, manage adjuster workloads, and reduce unnecessary costs [O0005].

### 3.8 Industry Intel

Industry Intel (Claims Intel) empowers P&C insurers to make faster, smarter claims decisions by providing intelligent predictions across the claims lifecycle. It is directly embedded in ClaimCenter workflows, delivering information at the point of decision [O0005]. Industry Intel draws from a pool of insurance data representing more than $326B in direct written premium, paired with Guidewire Predict to produce predictive scores [O0008].

## 4. Technology Platform

### 4.1 Guidewire Cloud

Guidewire Cloud is the company's cloud delivery platform, providing core modernization and AI transformation for P&C insurers. It is powered by AWS (Guidewire was named AWS 2023 Global Partner of the Year for Financial Services). Over 100 customers worldwide leverage Guidewire Cloud, with 164K deployments and 171M API calls [O0007] [O0007] [O0007].

Key capabilities include: operationalizing AI at scale through the Agentic Framework (AI Connect, MCP Servers, Cloud APIs), business agility through effortless market adaptation, trusted infrastructure with cloud-first hybrid architecture, and operational predictability with usage-based pricing [O0007].

Guidewire Cloud maintains rigorous security and compliance standards including SOC 1, 2, 3 and ISO 27001 certifications [O0007].

### 4.2 Insurance AI from Guidewire

Guidewire has built a comprehensive AI platform enabling insurers to build, deploy, and govern AI. The platform operates across three layers: agentic and predictive AI, insurance-specific context, and an open trusted core [O0008].

Key AI components include:

- **ProNavigator**: An AI assistant inside Guidewire applications that answers questions, recommends next steps, and completes routine actions using the insurer's own policies, guidelines, and data. Launched in 2026 [O0008] [O0001].

- **Agentic Framework**: Enables insurers to build, test, and deploy custom AI agents or integrate agents from other vendors. Runs in a secure, model-agnostic environment with audit tracing, evaluations, and data protection [O0008].

- **Developer Assistants**: Guidewire-aware AI tools connected through Model Context Protocol (MCP) that reduce development effort by up to 60%. Available for Gosu, Integrations, and Jutro Digital Platform [O0008] [O0008].

### 4.3 Jutro Digital Platform

Jutro is P&C insurance's first and only development platform for complete digital transformation [O0006].

### 4.4 Guidewire Data Platform

The Guidewire Data Platform unlocks the potential of insurer data [O0006].

The Guidewire Marketplace is a curated storefront offering pre-built, validated insurtech solutions and complementary applications, enabling insurers to rapidly deploy new capabilities without custom development. More than 175 partner applications are available in the Marketplace [O0004] [O0004].

## 5. Partner Ecosystem

Guidewire has created the largest ecosystem in the P&C insurance industry through its PartnerConnect program, which brings together more than 24,000 technology consultants, systems integrators, and solution providers [O0001].

The partner ecosystem is structured into several categories [O0009]:

- **Consulting Partners**: Systems integrators who implement complex solutions, providing enablement, training, and marketing support.
- **Technology Partners**: Innovators who build on Guidewire and develop pre-built products for the Guidewire Marketplace. More than 200 products and services are available.
- **Insurtech Vanguards**: A program that scouts and nurtures the next generation of insurtech innovators as potential new PartnerConnect members.
- **Guidewire Ventures**: A program supporting companies across the insurance ecosystem to develop and offer innovative insurance technology solutions.
