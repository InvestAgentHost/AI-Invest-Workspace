# Guidewire Software -- Strategy and Developments

## 1. Cloud Transformation Strategy

Guidewire's cloud transformation is the central strategic pillar of the company. CEO Mike Rosenbaum, who was named CEO in 2019, has spearheaded Guidewire's full transition to the cloud [O0001]. The cloud journey began in earnest with the Aspen release in 2020, the company's first cloud-optimized platform release, which officially reimagined insurance core platforms [O0001].

Guidewire Cloud is positioned as the platform for core modernization and AI transformation, designed to end the cycle of cumbersome upgrades and maintenance and empower IT to create strategic business value [O0007]. Over 100 customers around the world now leverage Guidewire Cloud, which is powered by AWS. Guidewire was named AWS 2023 Global Partner of the Year for Financial Services [O0007] [O0007].

The platform delivers continuous improvement through named releases. Since Aspen in 2020, Guidewire has shipped releases at a roughly quarterly cadence [O0007]:

- **2026**: Palisades (embedded intelligent assistants for PolicyCenter, ClaimCenter, and InsuranceNow) and Qusar (Agentic Framework and AI agents)
- **2025**: Olos (pricing modernization), Niseko (financial performance insight), Mammoth (personal/commercial lines improvements)
- **2024**: Las Lenas (risk selection and pricing), Kufri (HazardHub expansion to 19 countries, developer experience), Jasper (commercial lines agility)
- **2023**: Innsbruck, Hakuba, Garmisch
- **2022**: Flaine, Elysian (Embedded Insurance Solution)
- **2021**: Dobson (Guidewire Live), Cortina (Integration Framework)

Guidewire actively promotes migration to the cloud, noting benefits including fast updates (days rather than months), rapid market adjustment, operational predictability, and usage-based pricing [O0007].

## 2. AI and Machine Learning Initiatives

### 2.1 Insurance AI Platform

Guidewire has built a comprehensive AI platform centered on three layers: agentic and predictive AI, insurance-specific context, and an open trusted core. The company positions itself as "the platform P&C insurers use to build, deploy, and govern AI" [O0008].

AI is being embedded across the insurance lifecycle, automating routine work in quoting, underwriting, claims, and service. Guidewire asserts that first-mover insurers will operate at lower combined ratios and that the advantage compounds over time [O0008].

### 2.2 ProNavigator Launch (2026)

In 2026, Guidewire launched ProNavigator, an AI assistant embedded directly inside Guidewire applications. ProNavigator answers questions, recommends next steps, and completes routine actions such as updating claim records or drafting customer replies, all grounded in the insurer's own policies, guidelines, and data rather than the open web [O0008] [O0001].

### 2.3 Qusar Release and Agentic Framework (August 2026)

On August 3, 2026, Guidewire launched the Qusar release, introducing the Agentic Framework into Guidewire Cloud Platform. This framework enables insurers to build, deploy, and manage AI agents, supporting both Guidewire-built and custom/third-party agents [O0012].

Key Qusar innovations include [O0012]:

- **Claim Summarization for ProNavigator**: Provides adjusters with claim summaries so they can focus on complex resolutions (Restricted Availability)
- **Policy Change for ProNavigator**: An embedded assistant helping underwriters and customer service reps complete policy changes faster (Early Access)
- **Agentic First Notice of Loss (FNOL)**: Uses conversational AI voice to guide claimants through the first notice of loss process (Restricted Availability)

Qusar also introduced Developer and Builder Assistants that understand the Guidewire code base, programming languages, design patterns, and configurations, including assistants for Gosu, Integrations, Jutro, Functions, Data Studio (Data Curation Assistant), and Advanced Product Designer [O0012]. Guidewire reports that Developer Assistants help teams deliver solutions more than 40% faster than generic coding assistants [O0012] [O0012].

### 2.4 Quantee Acquisition (2025)

In 2025, Guidewire acquired Quantee to advance AI-driven pricing and underwriting capabilities [O0001].

### 2.5 ProNavigator Acquisition (2025)

Guidewire announced the acquisition of ProNavigator in 2025 to embed AI-powered insurance knowledge across the Guidewire platform, and launched the integrated ProNavigator product in 2026 [O0001] [O0001].

### 2.6 Industry Intel and Predict

Industry Intel harnesses a pool of insurance data from insurers representing more than $326B in direct written premium. Paired with Guidewire Predict, it produces predictive scores inside Guidewire applications used by underwriters and pricing teams [O0008]. Underwriters using Predict have achieved up to 20% growth in written premium [O0008].

## 3. Recent Product Releases and Platform Updates

### 3.1 Palisades Release (2026)

The Palisades release delivered elevated performance with embedded, intelligent assistants for PolicyCenter, ClaimCenter, and InsuranceNow [O0007].

### 3.2 Developer Assistants

Guidewire's Developer Assistants connect AI coding tools and IDEs to Guidewire through Model Context Protocol (MCP), enabling AI to understand the Guidewire platform's APIs, data model, and conventions. Early Access users report development effort reductions of up to 60% [O0008] [O0008].

### 3.3 HazardHub Expansion

Through the Kufri release in 2024, HazardHub was extended to 19 additional countries beyond its original U.S. coverage [O0007]. In September 2026, Guidewire HazardHub published findings that over 1 million California homes are at risk for extreme wildfires [O0001].
