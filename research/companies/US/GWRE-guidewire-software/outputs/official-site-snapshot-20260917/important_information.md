## 2. Corporate Sustainability Commitments

Guidewire publishes a comprehensive sustainability report covering environmental, social, governance, and product sustainability dimensions [O0011].

### 2.1 Sustainability Approach

The company's sustainability approach focuses on supporting the global P&C insurance industry, which it views as critical to society by helping people and businesses recover from loss and thrive. Guidewire works with its community of employees, customers, and partners to drive innovation and efficiency, strengthen resilience, and promote long-term sustainability [O0011].

The sustainability program is organized into the following pillars [O0011]:

- **Environmental**: Climate and energy, responsible resource management, and building climate resilience
- **Social**: Employee programs ("Our People"), community engagement ("Guidewire Gives Back"), and spotlight stories
- **Governance**: Governance and ethics, security and data privacy, responsible sourcing and procurement
- **Product Sustainability**: Product-enabling sustainability initiatives

### 3.2 Company History Milestones

Key milestones in Guidewire's history [O0001]:

- **2001**: Founded by six entrepreneurs
- **2003**: ClaimCenter launched as inaugural product; CNA and Hastings Mutual become first customers
- **2006**: PolicyCenter launches
- **2007**: BillingCenter becomes third core system, completing InsuranceSuite; Innovation Awards launched
- **2012**: IPO on NYSE under ticker GWRE
- **2014**: Named a leader in first-ever Gartner Magic Quadrant for P&C Core Platforms, North America
- **2017**: InsuranceNow launches (first cloud-based all-in-one core platform)
- **2019**: Mike Rosenbaum named CEO
- **2020**: Aspen (first cloud-optimized platform release)
- **2021**: HazardHub acquired; Insurtech Vanguards launched; celebrated 20 years
- **2022**: Global Services Center opened in Bengaluru, India; Dublin, Ireland becomes EMEA headquarters
- **2023**: Named AWS 2023 Global Financial Services ISV Partner of the Year
- **2024**: First DevSummit India, attracting over 500 IT professionals
- **2025**: Acquired Quantee; announced acquisition of ProNavigator
- **2026**: Launched ProNavigator; released Palisades and Qusar

### 3.3 Security and Compliance

Guidewire maintains compliance certifications and attestations including SOC 1, 2, 3, ISO 27001, and alignment with GDPR and other regulatory standards. The company maintains a Trust Center for customers [O0007] [O0003].

### 3.4 Global Offices

Guidewire is headquartered in San Mateo, California. The company has a Global Services Center in Bengaluru, India (opened 2022), an EMEA headquarters in Dublin, Ireland (designated 2022, with the Dublin Global Services Center having opened in 2011), and additional offices established over its history including London (2005) and Sydney (2005) [O0001] [O0001].
