## Corporate identity

Broadcom Inc. is headquartered in Palo Alto, California, and is incorporated in Delaware (having redomiciled from Singapore in 2018). Its stock trades on Nasdaq under the ticker AVGO [S010].

## Leadership (as of the most recent SEC disclosures)

- **Hock E. Tan** — President, Chief Executive Officer and Director (director since 2006) [S010] [S027]
- **Amie Thuener** — Chief Financial Officer, effective June 12, 2026 (succeeded Kirsten M. Spears, who retired the same date after six years as CFO/CAO; previously VP, Corporate Controller and Chief Accounting Officer of Alphabet Inc. since 2018) [S026] [S018] [E006]
- **Mark D. Brazeal** — Chief Legal and Corporate Affairs Officer [S010]
- **Charlie B. Kawwas, PhD** — President, Semiconductor Solutions Group [S010]

## Board of directors

Per the 2026 DEF 14A proxy statement (filed 2026-03-02, for the Annual Meeting held 2026-04-20), eight director nominees stood for election [S027] [E007]:

1. **Diane M. Bryant** — Independent Director; former CEO of NovaSignal Corp.; director since 2019
2. **Gayla J. Delly** — Independent Director; Audit Committee Chair; former President/CEO of Benchmark Electronics; director since 2017
3. **Kenneth Y. Hao** — Independent Director; Chairman and Managing Partner of Silver Lake; director since 2024
4. **Check Kian Low** — Independent Director; founding partner of NewSmith Capital Partners; director since 2016
5. **Justine F. Page** — Independent Director; former CFO of Integrated Circuit Systems, Inc.; director since 2019
6. **Henry Samueli, PhD** — Chairman of the Board (independent); co-founder of Broadcom Corporation; director since 2016, Chairman since 2018
7. **Hock E. Tan** — President and CEO (not independent); director since 2006
8. **Harry L. You** — Independent Director; Compensation Committee Chair; former CFO of GTY Technology Holdings; director since 2019

A ninth incumbent director, **Eddy W. Hartenstein** (Lead Independent Director), did not stand for re-election; he retired upon conclusion of his term at the 2026 Annual Meeting, having offered his resignation upon reaching age 75 per Broadcom's Corporate Governance Guidelines — explicitly not the result of any dispute with the company. The Board accordingly shrank from nine to eight members [S025] [E007].

## Governance structure

Broadcom's Chairman of the Board, Henry Samueli, is described in the proxy as "our independent Chairman of the Board," distinct from CEO Hock Tan, who holds the President/CEO/Director role but not the Chairman title [S027].

## Intellectual property and scale (governance-adjacent facts)

As of November 2, 2025: approximately 33,000 employees worldwide (~57% in R&D); approximately 19,000 U.S. and other patents and 2,170 pending patent applications [S010] [E002] [E003].
