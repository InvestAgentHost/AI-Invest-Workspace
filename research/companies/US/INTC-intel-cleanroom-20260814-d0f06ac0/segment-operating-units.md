## Representative economic cycle

A server CPU design begins with workload specifications and architecture, then consumes R&D before revenue. A platform must be qualified by customers; it then enters a chosen process node, where wafer starts become work-in-process inventory. Yield determines how many saleable dies emerge, while utilization determines how fixed fab depreciation and labor spread over output. Packaging/testing produces shippable units; control transfer creates revenue and receivables, and collection creates operating cash. A node delay or weak yield therefore hits the model three times: delayed product revenue, higher unit cost/Foundry loss, and more capital trapped in PP&E and WIP. If demand is weaker than planned, utilization falls and fixed cost remains; if demand is stronger but qualified capacity is constrained, Intel loses sales and customer trust. [S01][S03][S07][S14]
