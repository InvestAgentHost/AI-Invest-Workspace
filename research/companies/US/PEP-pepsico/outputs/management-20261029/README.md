At target operating performance, the0.75–1.25 TSR modifier changes the performance-stock portion by plus/minus15% of the total target grant value; it does not impose performance conditions on the40% service component. Talent retention and reduced incentive to manipulate a small set of metrics are plausible benefits, not demonstrated incremental economic returns. A more favorable judgment requires evidence of necessary retention, reasonable grant cost, demanding performance thresholds and shareholder value creation, including operating ROIC and cash returns.
