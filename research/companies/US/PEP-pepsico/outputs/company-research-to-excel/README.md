# PEP investment-research workbook

Current file: `research/companies/US/PEP-pepsico/outputs/company-research-to-excel/PEP_model.xlsx`.
Research layout updated 2026-10-22; actuals through FY2026Q3. All workbook text remains in English.

## Reading order

1. **Segments — Segment Operating Trends.** Three compact time-series tables compare the six businesses across FY2022–FY2025 and FY2024Q1–FY2026Q3, including Q4: revenue and profit, volume and pricing, and capital investment intensity. Corporate expenses and capital spending remain visible. GAAP and company-defined Core profit are distinct. Core profit and demand drivers begin in FY2023; early YoY comparisons remain blank without a matching prior period.
2. **Operating Metrics — Operating History and Key Drivers.** Meaningful FY2016–FY2025 annual trends and FY2024Q1–FY2026Q3 discrete quarters, including Q4. Retains the Frito-Lay / Quaker split, distinct legacy regional boundaries, category volume indicators and selected material charges. PBNA FY2022 onward is not duplicated in the legacy panel.
3. **Products & Geography.** Annual country concentration, long-lived assets versus the newer PP&E definition, food / beverage mix and company-owned bottling. Quarterly approximate shares and repeated cumulative panels have been removed.
4. **Financial statements and supporting schedules.** Use Income Statement, Balance Sheet, Cash Flow and Derived Metrics to connect operating performance to cash generation, operating ROIC, debt and shareholder returns. Economically useful note schedules remain.

## Interpretation

- Revenue and Core profit growth calculated from amounts can differ from rounded headline growth in the releases.
- Physical volume is distinct from the volume contribution to revenue growth. Effective net pricing includes pricing, promotions and mix.
- Reporting boundaries are not spliced across segment reorganizations. Current-six FY2022 Core profit is unavailable; no estimate is substituted. Restated FY2016–FY2017 consolidated Core profit remains in the ten-year trend.
- Q4 additive monetary amounts are reported or derived from matching annual and Q1–Q3 amounts. Growth rates and approximate shares are not subtracted.
- Capex / D&A describes investment intensity. It is not maintenance capex or operating ROIC. Quarter-to-quarter capital spending is seasonal.
- Blank means not applicable or not separately disclosed; a numerical zero is displayed as a dash.

Full segment adjustment matrices, repeated operating YTD views, undifferentiated narrative paragraphs, obsolete servings disclosures and the dedicated reconciliation-check section have been deleted from the workbook. Checks and source records stay outside the reader-facing operating schedules.

The unchanged original sources remain under `sources/companies/US/PEP-pepsico/`.
