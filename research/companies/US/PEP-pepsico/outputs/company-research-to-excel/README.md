## Proposed profit forecast — 2026-10-29

Forecasts are on the left; aligned annual and recent-quarter reference values are on the right. The original fourteen historical sheets retain their existing data, formulas and formatting.

Each segment has one compact calculation sequence: organic revenue growth → combined FX/scope/calendar effect → reported revenue → Core margin → Core operating profit. The consolidated bridge deducts corporate costs and includes pension, net interest/other, taxes and noncontrolling interests. Core parent net income is then bridged to a conditional reported-profit estimate using explicit restructuring, integration and event assumptions. Existing intangible amortization and equity-accounted contributions already inside segment profit are not added or deducted again.

## Three-case valuation — 2026-10-29

**Valuation Model** follows the user-provided valuation template: the present value of annual cash dividends plus the present value of FY2031 parent net income × exit FCF conversion × exit P/FCF, divided by ending actual shares. It is an equity cash-return model with an exit multiple, not a perpetual FCFF valuation. Do not also add all interim FCF or subtract net debt again. The FY2032 steady-growth input in `Profit Forecast!C4` does not feed this valuation.

The 15% required return in `Valuation Model!D10` is inherited from the user's template and is editable. Exit P/FCF is provisionally 18×/22×/26×, FCF conversion 80%/85%/90%, cash-dividend payout 75%, and annual net-share change 0%/−0.25%/−0.40% for pessimistic/base/optimistic. These are research assumptions in the accepted working model, not company guidance or a trading decision. Share change is annual, with a positive sign for issuance and negative for shrinkage. Beginning and exit conversion can be changed separately; the beginning rate affects the cash-coverage projection while the exit rate affects the terminal equity value.

The operating comparison changes only North America. Pessimistic assumes zero organic growth for PFNA/PBNA, base assumes 1% each, and both hold Core margins at 21.7%/10.8%. Optimistic retains the original operating forecast. The four international segments and all nonsegment profit-bridge inputs are unchanged. Consequently, the pessimistic case is a North America stagnation case, not a company-wide extreme stress. The resulting FY2026–FY2031 reported parent NI CAGRs are approximately 2.43%, 3.03% and 4.13%.

## Interpretation

- Revenue and Core profit growth calculated from amounts can differ from rounded headline growth in the releases.
- Physical volume is distinct from the volume contribution to revenue growth. Food weight and beverage cases are not averaged. Contribution rows are numerical percentage points (pp); growth rates are percentages. Effective net pricing includes pricing, promotions and mix. FY2017 and FY2022 consolidated volume contributions are issuer-rounded zeros, not missing data.
- Consolidated annual revenue-volume contributions for FY2016–FY2025 are 2, 0, 1, 0.5, 2, 4, 0, -3, -2 and -2 pp. These agree with the separately published revenue-attribution chart. FY2022 beverage physical volume growth of 3.5% is a different measure from its zero consolidated revenue-volume contribution.
- FX and M&A contribution signs in Segments express the effect on reported growth. The original Operating Metrics rows retain the inverse adjustment-to-organic signs. Rounding residuals have no independent operating interpretation and must not be attributed to volume or pricing.
- Reporting boundaries are not spliced across segment reorganizations. Current-six FY2022 Core profit is unavailable; no estimate is substituted. Restated FY2016–FY2017 consolidated Core profit remains in the ten-year trend.
- Q4 additive monetary amounts are reported or derived from matching annual and Q1–Q3 amounts. Growth rates and approximate shares are not subtracted.
- Capex / D&A describes investment intensity. It is not maintenance capex or operating ROIC. Quarter-to-quarter capital spending is seasonal.
- Blank means not applicable or not separately disclosed; a numerical zero is displayed as a dash.

Full segment adjustment matrices, repeated operating YTD views, undifferentiated narrative paragraphs, obsolete servings disclosures and the dedicated reconciliation-check section have been deleted from the workbook. Checks and source records stay outside the reader-facing operating schedules.
