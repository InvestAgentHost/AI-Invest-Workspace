## Limits

- Brand lists cannot establish brand-level revenue, margins or contribution to earnings.
- Nutrition-oriented positioning does not itself establish product health outcomes, pricing power or profitable incremental demand.
- Food and beverage pairings are possible consumption occasions; the maps do not assert mandatory bundling, identical delivery routes or quantified synergies.
- FY2025 10-K remains the primary ownership and business-boundary reference; current official product pages illustrate product-line depth, rather than recreating the FY2025 assortment.
