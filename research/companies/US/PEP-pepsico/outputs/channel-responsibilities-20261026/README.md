# PepsiCo channel responsibility diagrams

As of 2026-10-26. These are analytical illustrations of typical arrangements,
not disclosed channel shares or a reconstruction of any individual contract.

- `01-food-channel-responsibilities.png`: Direct supply with DSD, customer
  warehouse, and buy-resell distribution alternatives for foods.
- `02-company-owned-bottling-responsibilities.png`: Company-owned bottling with
  alternative downstream routes; not the entire PBNA reporting segment.
- `03-franchise-beverage-responsibilities.png`: Concentrate-franchise route with
  continuing brand-side support; excludes SodaStream.

Blue denotes PepsiCo's operating responsibility, including activities it may
outsource. Orange denotes independent distributors/bottlers; green denotes
retail/foodservice customers. Responsibility boundaries, title transfer,
merchandising and payment timing depend on the underlying contract.

## Evidence and interpretation

Primary source: PepsiCo FY2025 10-K, Item 1 (businesses, distribution network,
customers) and Note 2 (revenue and marketplace spending).

- Original URL: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/77476/000007747626000007/pep-20251227.htm
- Workspace original: `sources/companies/US/PEP-pepsico/artifacts/ten_k/PEP-pepsico/FY2025/source-http-body.html`
- Sources reused from the existing reviewed company package; no raw source edited.

The division into three pictured alternatives, responsibility bars and payment
arrows are analyst explanations of typical economic arrangements. They do not
establish a universal accounting cutoff at a physical delivery point. Payments
show settlement relationships, not a mandatory chronological order. Third-party
carriage alone does not imply an independent buy-resell distributor.

DSD is not restricted to PepsiCo employees. Independent distributor involvement
can coexist with DSD-like terminal service; shipment to a store alone does not
establish classification under PepsiCo's own reporting conventions.
