# Business and Offering Handbook

## Company Profile

Linde plc (NASDAQ/Nasdaq: LIN) describes itself as a leading global industrial gases and engineering company serving end markets including chemicals and energy, food and beverages, electronics, healthcare, manufacturing, and metals and mining [O0002]. The company reports 2025 sales of $34 billion [O0020]. Linde traces its corporate heritage to 1879, through predecessor companies Linde AG and Praxair, which completed a "merger of equals" in 2018 to form Linde plc [O0002:L109-L163].

## Industrial Gases

Linde's core gases business supplies industrial, specialty, and healthcare gases used "across just about every industry and country in the world," including life-saving oxygen for hospitals, food-grade carbon dioxide for beverage carbonation, high-purity and specialty gases for electronics manufacturing, and hydrogen for clean fuels [O0004:L9-L15]. The company organizes its gases offering around featured end-market industries such as construction, chemicals, aquaculture, pulp & paper, electronics, food & beverage, pharma & biotech, plastics & rubber, power & low-carbon fuels, metal fabrication, oil & gas upstream, glass, water treatment, refining, and metals [O0004:L43-L73].

Notable named gas-application technologies and services include:
- **ADDvance O2 precision** — atmosphere-control technology for additive manufacturing (3D printing) chambers, aimed at reducing material waste and improving part durability [O0004:L19-L23].
- **SOLVOX OxyStream** — energy-efficient oxygenation solutions for land-based aquaculture tanks, supporting more sustainable fish farming [O0004:L31-L35].
- **DIGIGAS** — remote monitoring technology that tracks gas cylinder contents to support automated inventory management and replenishment [O0004:L37-L41].

## Engineering

Linde Engineering positions itself as a partner for the production and processing of gases, citing more than 4,000 plant references worldwide and describing itself as offering "technology for every step in the hydrogen value chain – from source to service" [O0005:L7-L27]. The engineering business emphasizes decarbonization and efficiency technologies and long-standing customer/industry engagement to improve plant efficiency [O0005:L17-L21].

**LINDE PLANTSERV®** is the branded plant-lifecycle service offering, combining efficiency- and reliability-enhancing services with digital innovations across the asset lifecycle, drawing on the company's experience engineering more than 4,000 plants worldwide [O0005:L29-L33].

## Advanced Material Technologies (AMT)

Linde Advanced Material Technologies (AMT) supplies metallic and ceramic coatings, sputtering targets, additive-manufacturing metal powders, and coating equipment [O0006:L7-L17]. Named product lines include:
- **Copper physical vapor deposition (PVD) sputtering targets** for semiconductor thin-film manufacturing, supported by a 6N copper refining line using primarily recycled copper feedstock [O0006:L21-L25].
- **SERMETEL and EXOGARD chrome-free coating systems** — alternatives to hexavalent-chrome and hard-chrome plating for corrosion, erosion, and wear protection [O0006:L27-L31].
- **TAFA and Genie thermal spray equipment and accessories**, plus related maintenance services [O0006:L33-L37].
- **TRUFORM metal powders** for additive manufacturing, used in aerospace, medical, energy, and industrial applications [O0006:L39-L43].

AMT's featured end markets are aviation & aerospace, semiconductors & electronics, energy, and general industrial applications [O0006:L45-L61].

## Clean Energy and Hydrogen

Linde presents clean energy, and hydrogen specifically, as a core strategic growth area built on "over 100 years of experience" in gases and engineering, spanning carbon capture and hydrogen technologies, key alliances and ventures, and the company's existing infrastructure [O0011:L7-L13]. The company states it has been "harnessing the power of hydrogen for over 100 years" and is investing across the gray, blue, and green hydrogen pathways [O0012:L37-L43].

**Hydrogen value chain (production through distribution):**
- *Production* — Linde produces gray hydrogen via steam methane reforming (SMR) or partial oxidation from natural gas, LPG, or naphtha; blue hydrogen by combining SMR with carbon capture and storage; and green hydrogen via electrolysis powered by renewable energy or via SMR using bio-based feedstock. The company reports operating over 150 SMR/PSA units and 80+ electrolysis plants worldwide [O0013:L21-L31].
- *Processing* — Linde reports long-standing experience separating synthesis gas via cryogenic processes, and states it is the world leader in liquid hydrogen (LH2) production capacity at 170 tonnes per day, having built the majority of the world's hydrogen liquefaction plants [O0013:L39-L45].
- *Storage & Distribution* — Linde operates roughly 1,000 kilometers of hydrogen pipeline and what it describes as the largest fleet of hydrogen trailers in the world, cryogenic tanks ranging from 3,000 to over 100,000 liters, and the world's first commercial high-purity hydrogen storage cavern (operating in Texas for over a decade) [O0013:L51-L65].
- *One-stop-shop / Build-Own-Operate* — Linde offers an integrated production-to-supply model leveraging synergies between Linde Engineering and Linde Gas, including standardized **ECOVAR® on-site supply systems** for larger-volume hydrogen requirements [O0013:L73-L85].

**Hydrogen applications:** Linde markets hydrogen solutions across heavy industry, aviation, mobility (planes, trains, boats, and buses), and power buffering as a means of supporting renewable-power integration [O0014:L13-L37]. Cited collaborations include agreements with Snam (clean hydrogen project development) and Daimler Truck (hydrogen refueling technology) [O0014:L39-L47].

**Hydrogen technologies:** The company's named technology areas include electrolysis for green hydrogen production, hydrogen fueling technologies for cars, trucks, trains, forklifts, and buses, conversion of natural-gas grid infrastructure for hydrogen delivery, underground salt-cavern storage, and hydrogen liquefaction systems [O0015:L13-L47].

## Carbon Capture, Utilization and Storage (CCUS)

Linde supports customers across the "carbon management value chain," from capture and conditioning through transportation, utilization, and storage [O0016:L7-L11]. The company describes three principal capture strategies — pre-combustion, oxy-fuel combustion, and post-combustion — and states that integrating carbon capture into steam methane reforming can capture up to 95% of generated CO2, enabling blue hydrogen production [O0016:L19-L21] [O0016:L73-L77]. Target industries for CCUS include hydrogen and ammonia, refining, cement, steel, and chemicals [O0016:L69-L86]. Linde has partnered with BASF to deliver integrated post-combustion capture solutions, combining BASF's gas-treatment technology with Linde's engineering, project execution, and CO2-processing capabilities [O0016:L109-L121].

## Target End Markets

Across its business lines, Linde identifies its principal end markets as chemicals and energy, food and beverages, electronics, healthcare, manufacturing, and metals and mining [O0002:L9-L17], with additional emphasis on semiconductor/electronics customers as a growth driver reflected in significant capital commitments (see Strategy and Developments) [O0020:L11-L19].
