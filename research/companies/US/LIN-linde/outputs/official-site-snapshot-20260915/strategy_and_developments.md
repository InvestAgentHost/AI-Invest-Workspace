## Clean-Energy and Hydrogen Strategy

Linde frames the clean-energy transition, and hydrogen in particular, as a central strategic growth pillar, stating it is "playing a key role in the clean energy transition" by helping customers decarbonize through carbon capture and hydrogen technologies, alliances and ventures, and its existing infrastructure and experience [O0011:L7-L13]. The company positions hydrogen as "a key enabler of the transition to low and zero-carbon energy," citing the Paris Agreement and rising corporate/government carbon-neutrality commitments as demand drivers [O0012:L21-L33]. Linde states hydrogen could become the most competitive low-carbon solution in more than 20 applications by 2030, including long-haul trucking, shipping, and steel [O0012:L29-L33].

Strategically, Linde describes a staged pathway from gray to blue to green hydrogen, continuing investment across all three, while building out complementary carbon-capture (CCUS) capabilities — including a named collaboration with BASF on integrated post-combustion capture — as an enabler of blue hydrogen and broader industrial decarbonization [O0012:L37-L51] [O0016:L109-L121].

## Investor Value Proposition

Linde's investor-facing messaging (as of this snapshot) emphasizes:
- 2025 sales of $34 billion [O0020:L9-L11].
- Helping customers avoid more than 2x Linde's own greenhouse gas emissions through its products and services [O0020:L13-L15].
- A stated commitment to growing EPS, on average, more than 10% every year [O0020:L26-L27].
- Recognition as a "dividend aristocrat" with 32 consecutive years of raising the dividend [O0020:L28-L29].
- A claimed focus on increasing network density through an integrated supply model, which the company says has produced the highest return on capital (ROC) in the industry [O0020:L23-L25].
- More than 50 years of experience in hydrogen, leveraged alongside engineering capabilities and asset base to pursue clean-energy opportunities [O0020:L20-L21].

## Sustainability Strategy and Targets

Linde's sustainability strategy is described as central to its long-term investor proposition. Stated targets and ambitions include a "35 by 35" absolute greenhouse gas emissions reduction target for 2035 (announced October 2021) and a 2050 climate-neutrality ambition [O0019:L19-L21]. In 2025, the company reports having enabled customers to avoid approximately 98 million metric tons of CO2-equivalent emissions, while its own operations avoided 1 million metric tons of CO2e through efficiency efforts [O0018:L9-L11]. Low-carbon power is reported at around 50% of Linde's global electricity consumption as of 2025, up from a 2021 baseline, with active renewable power purchasing having grown 2.7x over that period (from 2.8 TWh to 7.6 TWh) [O0023:L11-L13].

Per the research plan's scope, the development window is set to 24 months; all items below fall within 2026 and are the only fully detailed, dated news items available in this snapshot (the news-and-media index itself, O0021, was a low-content dynamic page waived as non-material — see quality_report.json):

1. **July 31, 2026 — $1 billion U.S. semiconductor facility expansion investment.** Linde announced a new long-term agreement to supply ultra-high-purity industrial gases to one of the world's largest semiconductor manufacturers, investing $1 billion to expand its on-site industrial gases complex in Phoenix, Arizona, including two new SPECTRA® air separation units. 2. **July 27, 2026 — Renewable energy sourcing expansion (EMEA/APAC).** Linde signed six new power purchase agreements (PPAs) covering Spain, Greece, South Africa, and India, adding approximately 0.63 TWh/year of renewable energy, explicitly tied to progress toward its 35%-by-2035 absolute emissions reduction target [O0023].

3. **June 1, 2026 — Publication of the 2025 Sustainable Development Report.** Linde reported a 10% reduction in absolute greenhouse gas emissions versus its 2021 baseline, a rise in low-carbon/renewable electricity share to 50%, conservation of over one billion gallons of water, diversion of 200 million pounds of waste from landfills, and community engagement reaching roughly 2,100 organizations and nearly 400,000 people [O0024].

4. **May 5, 2026 — Dow Jones Best-in-Class and S&P Global sustainability recognition.** Linde was included in the Dow Jones Best-in-Class Indices for the 23rd consecutive year (98th percentile CSA score) and named to the S&P 2026 Sustainability Yearbook (848 of more than 9,200 eligible companies qualified) [O0025].
