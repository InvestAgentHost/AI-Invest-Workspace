# Important Information

Titles, structures, and mission statements are as self-reported by the company and were not independently verified against proxy statements or other third-party filings.

## Corporate Governance

Linde states it works to "continuously improve our corporate governance process to better serve the interests of all stakeholders," with interactions between management, employees, and business partners expected to be transparent, respectful, and consistent with the company's Code of Business Integrity (CBI) [O0008:L9-L11]. The governance structure as presented on the site comprises:
- A **Board of Directors** providing strategic oversight of the businesses and functions [O0008:L13-L17].
- **Board Committees** providing oversight of key aspects of the businesses and functions [O0008:L19-L23].
- Additional corporate governance documents and policies, available for download [O0008:L25-L29].

The company also discloses a formal channel for shareholder communications with the Board, including a UK phone number and mailing address (Forge, 43 Church Street West, Woking, Surrey, GU21 6HT, United Kingdom), noting that such communications may be confidential or anonymous [O0008:L31-L51].

## Vision, Mission and Values

Linde's stated mission is "Making Our World More Productive," working toward a vision "to be the best performing global industrial gases and engineering company, where our people deliver innovative and sustainable solutions for our customers in a connected world" [O0009:L9-L15]. The company organizes its stated values as follows [O0009:L17-L49]:
- **Safety** — a goal of "no harm to people, communities or the environment," with an emphasis on continuous improvement of safety culture and performance worldwide [O0009:L19-L23].
- **Integrity** — commitment to acting with integrity at all times, anchored in the company's Code of Business Integrity [O0009:L25-L33].
- **Community** — commitment to improving the communities where Linde lives and works through charitable contributions and employee volunteerism [O0009:L35-L39].
- **Inclusion** — embracing inclusion to attract, develop, and retain talent and build high-performing teams [O0009:L41-L45].
- **Accountability** — holding the organization accountable, individually and collectively, for both what it accomplishes and how [O0009:L47-L49].

## Corporate Heritage

Linde describes "more than 140 years of technological progress" tracing back to Carl von Linde's 1879 founding of Gesellschaft für Linde's Eismaschinen, through the histories of both Linde AG and Praxair (a former Linde division spun off from Union Carbide), culminating in the 2018 "Merger of Equals" of Praxair and Linde to form the combined company named Linde plc [O0002:L75-L163].

## Sustainability Mission and Targets

Linde states it has pursued a "pragmatic ESG approach" for more than two decades, reporting that it helps customers avoid more than double the emissions the company generates across its own global footprint, across a workforce the company describes as more than 65,000 employees [O0017:L9-L11]. In 2025, the company reports enabling customers to avoid approximately 98 million metric tons of CO2-equivalent emissions, while its own operations avoided 1 million metric tons of CO2e [O0018:L9-L11].

Stated sustainability priorities include Climate Change, People & Community, Integrity & Compliance, and Safety, Health and Environment [O0017:L61-L69]. Formal targets referenced on the site include "Sustainable Development 2028 Targets" across key priority areas, and the "35 by 35" absolute greenhouse gas emissions reduction target for 2035 (announced October 2021) together with a 2050 climate-neutrality ambition [O0019:L19-L21]. Linde reports recognition in third-party sustainability benchmarks, including inclusion in the Dow Jones Best-in-Class Indices for the 23rd consecutive year and the S&P 2026 Sustainability Yearbook [O0025:L11-L15].
