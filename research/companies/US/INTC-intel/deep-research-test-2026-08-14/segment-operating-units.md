# Segment routing and operating units

| Segment | Route | Economic unit | Revenue / cash event | Capital anchor | Risk carrier | Preferred KPI | Valuation route |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| CCPG | Manufacturing/hardware | client platform shipment | product control transfers; collections follow shipment | inventory, product R&D, internal/external wafer charges | Intel Products | volume, ASP/mix, gross/operating margin, inventory, warranty; volume/ASP unavailable | normalized EBIT/FCF |
| DCAI | Manufacturing/hardware plus custom silicon | server/accelerator/networking product or ASIC program | shipment or development/delivery milestone | R&D, inventory, wafer/packaging capacity | Intel Products | server volume, ASP/mix, hyperscaler/custom ASIC pipeline; detailed KPIs unavailable | normalized EBIT/FCF |
| Intel Foundry | Asset-heavy manufacturing/foundry | wafer, assembly/test milestone, packaging service | wafer-sort ownership transfer or assembly/test milestone | PP&E, process R&D, capacity and yield ramp | Intel Foundry and consolidated Intel | internal/external revenue, external mix, node volume, yield, utilization, committed demand | DCF/SOTP only after external demand and normalized economics are evidenced |
| All Other | Mixed hardware/investment | Mobileye/IMS/start-up output | segment-specific | investments, intangibles, working capital | relevant entity and NCI | separate company-level KPIs; not developed here | SOTP only with component evidence |
| Corporate / eliminations | Group bridge | allocation and internal transfer | no external customer event | corporate R&D/restructuring; internal pricing | parent and shareholders | unallocated cost, elimination consistency | subtract holding/unallocated cost; avoid double count |

Excluded modules: lender, insurer, resource, pharma, real estate, marketplace and recurring-service modules are economically inapplicable. Software ecosystem effects are discussed as product support, not routed as a standalone SaaS segment because recurring software revenue is not separately evidenced.

## Foundry boundary

- FY2025 Foundry revenue was $17.826bn, but $17.683bn of group intersegment revenue was eliminated at consolidation and direct external Foundry revenue was only $307m. The measures are not identical but establish that reported Foundry revenue is overwhelmingly internal [S01: segment table and segment definition].
- Q2 2026 Foundry revenue was $5.765bn, including $293m external revenue; H1 external revenue was $467m. Management attributed most of the year-over-year external increase to Altera becoming an external customer after deconsolidation, not to an independently demonstrated new leading-edge customer win [S02: Intel Foundry MD&A].
- Intel discloses node mix and qualitative yield/customer milestones but not numerical yield, fab utilization, external backlog, binding committed wafer demand, or customer-level 14A commitments. These remain `unavailable`.
