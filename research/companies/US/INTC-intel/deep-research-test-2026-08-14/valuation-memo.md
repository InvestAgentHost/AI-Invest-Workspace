# Valuation memo: PARTIAL

## Method decision

Intel is a heterogeneous product-plus-foundry group. A single P/E is invalid because FY2025 parent earnings were negative and H1 2026 parent loss was $14.761bn; the latter includes a $13.619bn non-cash mark-to-market loss on U.S. government Escrowed Shares but operating income was also negative for H1. The positive-net-income P/E/IRR solver is therefore `pending`, not forced.

The economically preferred future method is SOTP: normalized after-tax EBIT/FCF for CCPG/DCAI; a DCF or asset/customer-backed value for Foundry; separate values for All Other and investments; then cash, investments, debt, leases, NCI, corporate costs, dilution and government-share obligations. It cannot be bound today because numerical yield, utilization, committed external demand, maintenance/growth capex and normalized Foundry economics are unavailable.

## Reverse requirement

At an illustrative $566bn enterprise boundary, a no-growth terminal EV/EBIT range of 12x-18x implies $31bn-$47bn of sustainable EBIT. This is only a scale test: it is far above FY2025 consolidated operating loss and Q2 2026 annualized operating profit, so the market price embeds substantial recovery, growth, asset value or strategic optionality. The test must not be read as a target price because it omits growth duration, tax, working capital and capital intensity.
