## Governance

Since 1874, the company states it has "conducted business with honesty and integrity" [O0009]. All employees, officers, directors, and engaged third parties "share the same responsibility to report any violation of the company's policies or any illegal or unethical act," explicitly covering harassment, insider-information misuse, bribery, antitrust non-compliance, inaccurate books/records, and improper international activities [O0009]. The Governance page links to Board of Directors, Board Committees & Charters, Stockholder Contacts, and Policies & Procedures sub-pages (not independently crawled in this run) [O0009].

The company's Sustainability/Policies page lists specific governance-adjacent policies by name: Anti-Bribery Policy, Conflicts of Interest Policy, Insider Trading Policy, Financial Code of Ethics, Whistleblower Procedure, CA Transparency in Supply Chains Act statement, Conflict Minerals Statement, Conflict Minerals Report, and Human Rights Statement [O0010].

## Corporate values

The company states its values, in place "since 1874," are: Achieve Profitable Growth, Emphasize Innovation, Preserve Its Good Name, Be a Good Place to Work, and Be a Good Citizen [O0003].

## Intellectual property

The company maintains virtual patent marking pages (per the America Invents Act and 35 U.S.C. §287(a)) for the American Water Heater, A. O. Smith, A. O. Smith Retail, Lochinvar, Reliance, State, and Takagi brands, each linking to a dedicated patent list document [O0006].

## Sustainability / ESG

The company states it "embraces transparency by disclosing to CDP" and publishes an annual Sustainability Report and a "2025 Sustainability Progress Report & Scorecard" [O0010] [O0001].
