# Business and Offering Handbook

All content below is company-reported (sourced from www.aosmith.com) and should be treated as COMPANY_CLAIM, not independently verified fact, unless cross-checked separately against SEC filings.

## Company overview

A. O. Smith Corporation describes itself as "one of the world's leading water heating and water treatment solutions providers" [O0001]. The company was founded in 1874 in Milwaukee, Wisconsin, where it remains headquartered, and states it employs approximately 11,500 people across operations in the United States, Bangladesh, Canada, China, India, Mexico, the Netherlands, Sri Lanka and the United Kingdom, with sales and distribution in more than 60 countries [O0002].

## Core business areas

### Water Heating
The company states it offers "the broadest range of high-efficiency solutions" in water heating, spanning heat pump, gas, electric and tankless water heaters for residential and commercial customers [O0001] [O0007].

### Water Treatment
A. O. Smith describes its water treatment offerings as covering filtration and softening, "engineered to provide ... cleaner, safer water" [O0001] [O0007].

## Brand portfolio

A. O. Smith operates a multi-brand portfolio. Brands with dedicated virtual patent marking pages (indicating they are operated/owned by A. O. Smith or its subsidiaries) include: A. O. Smith, American Water Heater, Lochinvar, Reliance, State Water Heaters, and Takagi [O0006]. The company's brand page additionally lists Aquasana, Water-Right, Master Water, Hague, Watercare, Mineral-Right, Atlantic Filter, Clean Water Testing, Giant, GSW, Heat-Timer, Impact Water Products, John Wood, Leonard Valve, and U.S. Craftmaster among its featured/all-brand offerings [O0007].

Lochinvar, LLC is called out separately with a dedicated senior operating role ("Senior Vice President, President - Lochinvar, LLC"), indicating it is run as a distinct operating subsidiary within the group [O0005] [O0007].

Leonard Valve Company LLC is likewise called out with a dedicated senior operating role ("Senior Vice President and President of Leonard Valve Company LLC") [O0005]; the company's newsroom states A. O. Smith completed a $470 million acquisition of Leonard Valve Company on January 6, 2026, "expand[ing the] company's presence in [the] water management market" [O0008].

## Global operations footprint

The company's "About Us" page lists a detailed set of manufacturing, sales, R&D and distribution locations organized by region [O0002]:

- **North America**: corporate headquarters and Corporate Technology Center in Milwaukee, Wisconsin; North America Water Heating (NAWH) business-unit headquarters in Ashland City, Tennessee; a Canadian NAWH headquarters/distribution center in Fergus, Ontario; and numerous additional U.S. and Canadian sites for water-heater components, water-treatment products, pump/pressure tanks, boilers, valves, and a North America Product Development Center in Lebanon, Tennessee.
- **Europe & Middle East**: sites in Banbury (UK), Bourges and Lyon (France), Veldhoven (Netherlands), and Dubai (UAE), covering boilers, water heaters, heat pumps, solar thermal equipment and water-treatment sales.
- **Asia & Pacific**: sites in Bengaluru and Mumbai (India, including R&D), Colombo (Sri Lanka), Dhaka (Bangladesh), Ho Chi Minh City (Vietnam), Hong Kong SAR (Asian export/strategic planning), and Nanjing, China (China corporate headquarters and Corporate Technology Center, covering reverse-osmosis filtration and residential/commercial water heaters and kitchen appliances).

## Corporate values (as company-stated positioning)

The company states its values, unchanged "since 1874," are: Achieve Profitable Growth, Emphasize Innovation, Preserve Its Good Name, Be a Good Place to Work, and Be a Good Citizen [O0003].
